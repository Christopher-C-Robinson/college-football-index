How these rankings work Neutral matchups · full field

Every eligible FBS and FCS team faces every other eligible team on a neutral field, using the current forecast model. A team's score is its average forecast win chance across that shared schedule.

The forecast accounts for opponent-adjusted team strength, conference evidence, and passing/rushing matchups. Neutral games have no home-field advantage. Filters keep each team's overall rank.

A lower-ranked team can still be favored in an individual game because of its style or the venue. The rankings measure performance against the entire field.

Separate proof from projection

Power ratings use adjusted scoring. Résumé scores use opponent quality and actual wins versus expected wins. Efficiency is a distinct box-score lens.

FBS AND FCS RESULTS BOTH COUNT IN THE MODEL
C

Show data coverage

Every team shows its rated FBS/FCS results and usable box-score coverage separately. These counts describe the available data; they are not a confidence probability.

NO AP POLL OR COACHES POLL INPUTS

Forecasts combine opponent-adjusted team strength, conference information, home/away effects, and passing/rushing matchups. The matchup effects are fitted to historical pregame results, with no poll inputs. Missing unit data falls back to power + venue. Neutral sites receive no venue adjustment. The score total uses team scoring and points allowed; the matchup adjustment changes the scoring split. Injuries and weather are not modeled.

Venue and uncertainty details

Home and road effects are estimated from opponent-adjusted scoring residuals over five seasons, with recent seasons weighted more and small samples pulled toward the field average. The simulator combines the host’s home effect with the visitor’s road effect. Teams with fewer results receive wider rating uncertainty. Historical outcome ranges still cover fewer games than their stated 80% target; those ranges remain exploratory.

Data source & imports

The public snapshot loads automatically and covers FBS + FCS. You can also explore a season file of your own.

How the public snapshot is updated
1Get a free CFBD API key from CollegeFootballData.com.
2Run the local sync script. It loads the current season and cached history for home-road estimates and preseason projections.
3Load the generated JSON here, or publish that file with the static site so every visitor sees the same data.

Data source: CollegeFootballData.com API. The scheduled refresh caches six seasons of schedules to support five-season home-field estimates and a prior-season preseason fallback, plus current-season team metadata and box scores. The key stays in the GitHub Actions secret store.
