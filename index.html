03 / THE PLAYBOOK

Show the work.

The model explains how it gets to a number, what it leaves out, and when the sample is too small to trust.

See the data recipe ↗
A

Build the opponent graph

Every completed FBS and FCS game links two teams. Cross-division games connect the schedules, so results can be compared on one field.

HOME FIELD: 2.5 PTS · MARGIN CAP: 28 PTS · PRIOR: 2 GAMES
B

Separate proof from projection

Power ratings use adjusted scoring. Résumé scores use opponent quality and actual wins versus expected wins. Efficiency is a distinct box-score lens.

FBS AND FCS RESULTS BOTH COUNT IN THE MODEL
C

Show the evidence depth

Every team gets a sample-size indicator based on games and available box scores. Early-season ratings stay visibly provisional.

NO AP POLL OR COACHES POLL INPUTS

This is a transparent starting model, not a claim that one formula captures the whole sport. What-If simulations use adjusted power for the spread, the selected venue, scoring and points-allowed rates for the total, and wider rating uncertainty for teams with fewer results. They do not model injuries, weather, or matchup-specific play styles.
