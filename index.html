NO AP POLL OR COACHES POLL INPUTS

This is a transparent starting model, not a claim that one formula captures the whole sport. Home and road effects are estimated from opponent-adjusted scoring residuals over five seasons, with recent seasons weighted more and small samples pulled toward the field average. The team-page home-road swing is the estimated difference between a team's home and road performance; the simulator combines the host's home effect with the visitor's road effect. Neutral-site games receive no venue adjustment. Matchup simulations use adjusted power for the spread, scoring and points-allowed rates for the total, and wider rating uncertainty for teams with fewer results. The simulation ranges are exploratory until historical pregame backtests establish their coverage. Injuries, weather, and matchup-specific play styles are not modeled.
